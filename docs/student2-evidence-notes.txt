## Evidence provenance and interpretation

- The saved [market 463042 discovery response](../market_data/prediction_markets/fixtures/market-463042-response.json)
  has no recorded retrieval timestamp. It is historical discovery evidence,
  not a fresh quote or evidence of usable order-book depth. Its fixed window
  and split tie payout differ from a rolling strict-up target.
- A validated streamed book event binds the recorded levels and quote summary
  to the event timestamp; that timestamp does not prove when each resting order
  was created, guarantee that a displayed price is executable, or establish
  calibration. A REST listing retrieval time does not timestamp its quote, and
  the REST order book does not document a quote-freshness guarantee.
- A model can use a valid market with a different window, threshold or tie rule
  as evidence for an explicitly specified target while disclosing the differences
  and assumptions. Its answer is an experimental model estimate, separate from
  the observed YES payout midpoint. Numerical equality with that midpoint is not
  itself a validation failure, but mechanical copying is not a forecasting method.
  Required nonblank target, contract-difference and assumption explanations do
  not independently establish that a model's statements are true or calibrated.
- The strict quote inspector and the market-evidence model use the same market
  information and must not be counted as independent votes. Missing, stale,
  invalid or insufficient evidence remains grounds for abstention.
- The saved Mood, News and fake-headline examples are historical artifacts. The
  headline was synthetic; the paired comparison is not proof of general
  robustness. Retrospective model estimates do not establish prospective accuracy.
  Evaluate saved predictions against held-out outcomes using the declared source,
  timestamps and settlement convention before claiming an improvement.
